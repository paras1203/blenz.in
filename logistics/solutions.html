What Is LOaaS

Your operational office
in India — without
setting one up.

LOaaS — Logistics Office as a Service — is a managed operational infrastructure model that enables global companies to enter and operate in India without setting up a legal entity, hiring a local team, or investing in physical infrastructure.

Through LOaaS, Blenz functions as your on-ground operational arm. We take ownership of compliance, vendor relationships, customs execution, and logistics coordination — while you retain full commercial control and strategic direction.

This is not freight forwarding. It is not a courier arrangement. LOaaS is a structured, retainer-based partnership designed for businesses that want to grow in India without the complexity that typically comes with it.

"India is one of the world's most significant emerging markets — and one of its most operationally complex. LOaaS gives you the infrastructure to act like you're already here, without being here."

01

No legal entity required

Operate in India commercially without incorporation — LOaaS provides the operational and compliance layer that makes this possible.

02

Plug-and-play activation

LOaaS integrates into your existing business model. No infrastructure build-out, no long ramp-up — operational from the first engagement cycle.

03

Full-stack operational scope

From regulatory compliance and customs to warehousing, last-mile, and buyer relationships — LOaaS covers the entire operational surface area.

04

Aligned incentives

We operate as a strategic partner, not a vendor. Our engagement model is structured so that your growth is our growth.

How We Engage

A model built on
aligned incentives.

LOaaS engagements are structured in three layers. Every layer is designed to ensure that Blenz's commercial interest is directly aligned with your outcomes.

01

Retainer

A monthly management fee covering strategic oversight, compliance management, vendor and buyer relationships, and operational coordination. This is the foundation of the engagement — it ensures continuity, accountability, and dedicated resource allocation to your business.

02

Execution

Transactional costs — freight, customs duties, warehousing, and third-party fees — are billed at actuals, passed through with full transparency. No hidden markup. You see every line item. This keeps the cost structure honest and your landed cost calculations accurate.

03

Revenue Share

For select engagements where Blenz actively drives buyer introductions, distributor development, or commercial relationships, a performance-based revenue share can be structured alongside or in place of part of the retainer. We grow when you grow.

Common Entry Points

Typically Start.

There is no single path to India. But most engagements begin with one of three commercial objectives.

Testing India Without Entity Setup

The most common starting point. A global company has demand or intent in India but is not ready to incorporate. LOaaS provides the operational and compliance framework to execute commercially without a legal entity — using Blenz as the operational vehicle.

02

Using India as a Sourcing Hub

Businesses identify India as a competitive sourcing market but lack the ground infrastructure to access it reliably. LOaaS handles supplier onboarding, procurement structure, quality coordination, and export logistics — creating a dependable sourcing channel.

03

Building India Distribution

A brand or manufacturer is ready to establish a distribution presence in India. LOaaS activates warehousing, manages customs clearance, and facilitates introductions to the buyer and distributor network — compressing the market entry timeline significantly.
