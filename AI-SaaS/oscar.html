Contact management,

Core Features

Everything you need to
manage your professional network.

◉

Business Card Scanner

Point your camera at any business card. Oscar reads the text instantly, on your device, and creates a structured contact — no manual typing.

✦

AI Contact Enhancement

Oscar uses AI to clean, correct, and complete the information on a scanned card — so your contacts are accurate from day one.

⊞

Contact Organisation

Search, filter, and access your contacts with ease. Notes, tags, and a clean view — so you always find who you're looking for.

↗

One-Tap Actions

Call, email, visit a website, or message someone directly from the contact screen. No context-switching.

⇄

Share & Export

Share individual contacts via WhatsApp, SMS, vCard, or email. Export all contacts as a spreadsheet — for Pro users.

☁

Cloud Backup & Restore

Your contact data syncs securely to the cloud. Pro users can restore their entire library if they switch devices.

Who It's For

Built for anyone who
lives by their network.

Whether you're collecting ten cards at a conference or managing hundreds of professional relationships, Oscar keeps your contact data clean, structured, and useful.

01

No manual entry

Scan a card instead of typing out every field. Saves time and reduces errors, especially when you're collecting several at once.

02

AI cleans the data

OCR isn't perfect. Oscar uses AI to fix what it misses — so your contacts don't have garbled names or incomplete numbers.

03

Your data, your device

Scanned card images are never uploaded. Only the extracted contact text is stored. You can delete everything at any time.

04

Referral rewards

Share Oscar with your network. When they join, both of you benefit — with bonus scans or free Pro access.
